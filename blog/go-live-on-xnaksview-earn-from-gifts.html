How to Go LIVE on XnaksView and Earn From Gifts

By Baloch Sahab Technologies team · 9 November 2026 · 6 min read

XnaksView is our short-video and LIVE streaming app. Anyone can watch, but the people who get the most out of it are creators who go LIVE regularly, build a small community and receive gifts from their fans. This guide walks through the whole path: getting ready, starting your first LIVE, how gifts work, and how the money reaches your bank.

Everything here describes how the app works today. Rules can change as the app grows, and the app itself always shows the current requirement before you start.

Before your first LIVE

You need three things before the Go LIVE button opens for you.

  • An account with a real profile. Add a clear profile photo, a display name people can remember and a short bio that says what your LIVE is about: singing, chatting, gaming, cooking, studying together or anything else.
  • At least 10 followers. This small threshold keeps brand-new throwaway accounts from flooding the LIVE list. Post a few short videos, follow people in your niche and invite friends; ten followers usually comes quickly.
  • A stable connection. LIVE video is sensitive to network drops. Wi-Fi or a strong 4G signal is better than a weak connection that keeps reconnecting.

A quiet room, good light in front of you (not behind you) and a phone stand make a bigger difference than an expensive phone.

Starting a LIVE, step by step

  1. Open the app and tap the + button in the bottom bar.
  2. Choose LIVE and write a short, honest title. "Late night chat, ask me anything" tells viewers exactly what to expect.
  3. Check the camera preview, then tap Go LIVE.
  4. Greet people by name as they join. The app shows each new viewer, and a simple "welcome, glad you're here" turns visitors into regulars.

Keep your first sessions to 30 to 60 minutes. A shorter LIVE where you stay energetic is better than a long one where you run out of things to say.

Guests, co-hosts and PK battles

XnaksView LIVE rooms are not limited to one person on screen. As a host you can invite guests onto the stage in multi-seat layouts, so a LIVE can feel like a small show with friends rather than a one-person broadcast.

PK battles are one of the most popular formats. Two hosts are matched side by side for a timed round, and the gifts each one receives during the round decide the winner. Fans of both sides take part, which makes PK sessions some of the busiest moments in the app. Use them when you already have a few regular supporters, and agree on a friendly, fun tone with the other host.

How gifts work

Viewers buy coins and spend them on animated gifts during a LIVE. Small gifts cost only a few coins; large animated gifts cost more and play a full-screen animation for everyone in the room.

When you receive a gift, its value is added to your account as diamonds. Diamonds are your LIVE rewards. You can:

  • Exchange them for coins, if you want to send gifts to other creators yourself.
  • Withdraw them to your bank account as real money in your local currency.

Fans who send gifts climb gifter levels, can join your fan team, and appear on the hourly, daily, weekly and monthly rankings. Thank your top supporters by name; recognition is the main reason people keep coming back to the same host.

Viewers can buy coins inside the app through Google Play, or on our website, where the same coins cost 10% less. If a fan asks how to support you, point them to the in-app Balance page or the website.

Withdrawing your earnings safely

Withdrawals are where an earning app proves whether it is real, so we built this part carefully.

  • Identity verification (KYC). Before your first exchange or withdrawal, you verify your identity with the front and back of your ID card. This is required by payment partners and protects you from someone else cashing out your rewards.
  • Email confirmation. Every withdrawal, and every change to your payout method, is confirmed with a code sent to your email. If someone ever gets into your phone, they still cannot redirect your money without your email.
  • Payout to your own account. You add your bank details once in the payout settings. Bank fees and exchange rates are set by your bank.

Never share your verification codes with anyone, including people who claim to be from XnaksView support. Our team will never ask for them.

Earning from short videos too

LIVE gifts are not the only income. Creators who reach 1,000 followers and 10,000 video views in the last 28 days become eligible for a share of the ad revenue earned on their videos. Short videos also work as free advertising for your LIVE: a good clip brings new followers, and new followers show up the next time you go LIVE.

A simple rhythm that works for many creators:

  • Post one or two short videos a day.
  • Go LIVE at the same time on the same days, so people know when to find you.
  • Cut the best moment of each LIVE into a short video for the next day.

What gets accounts restricted

XnaksView is a public platform with people of all backgrounds, so a few rules are strictly enforced. Nudity and sexual content, and abuse or harassment of other users, lead to removal and can end in a ban. Respect other hosts in PK battles, keep jokes friendly and moderate your own chat; you can appoint trusted fans as moderators to help.

Staying inside the rules is also good for income. Viewers and gifters return to rooms where they feel comfortable.

Tips from what we see working

  • Consistency beats talent. Hosts who show up on a schedule grow faster than hosts who are occasionally brilliant.
  • Talk to people, not to the camera. Read comments aloud and answer them. A LIVE is a conversation.
  • Set a small goal. "Let's reach the next gift level together" gives fans a reason to take part.
  • Join an agency if you want structure. LIVE agencies on XnaksView bring creators together as a team, with guidance and shared events.

Getting started

Download XnaksView from Google Play, set up your profile, post a few short videos and reach your first ten followers. Then pick a time, write an honest title and go LIVE. Your first session does not need to be perfect; it only needs to happen.

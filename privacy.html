Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains how Baloch Sahab Technologies (SMC-Private) Limited (“we”, “us”) handles personal information on this website, balochsahab.com. Our individual products (such as the Baloch Sahab Earning Platform, EarnBox and XnaksView) have their own privacy policies, which apply when you use them.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you use the contact or quote form, or email us, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, email address, company, phone number and message.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting and security providers process technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, to deliver and protect the site.
  • Advertising cookies. This website uses Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads where permitted. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services and manage ad personalisation at Google Ad Settings. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), a consent message is shown before such cookies are used.

We do not use separate analytics tracking on this website.

How we use information

  • To reply to your message or prepare a quote you requested.
  • To operate, secure and improve this website.
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run this website: GitHub Pages (hosting), Cloudflare (network and security), FormSubmit (delivering form submissions to our email inbox), Google Fonts (web fonts) and Google AdSense (advertising). They process data on our behalf or under their own policies, and may process it outside Pakistan.

Retention

We keep inquiry emails for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a business record, and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your inquiry at any time.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete the personal information you have sent us, or object to its use, by emailing info@balochsahab.com. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

About Us

A technology company, built product by product.

Baloch Sahab Technologies started with a single earning website and grew into a company building web platforms, mobile apps and software for clients.

Our story

Baloch Sahab Technologies began as a single web project: an earning platform for everyday users. As that platform grew, so did the need for a proper company behind it, one that could take on mobile apps, take on client projects, and be accountable under its own legal name.

In August 2026 the company was formally incorporated in Pakistan with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) as an SMC-Private Limited company, a single-member company limited by shares. In November 2026 we also registered Baloch Sahab Technologies Ltd in England and Wales with Companies House, so we can serve international clients and app stores directly. Together the two companies are behind the Baloch Sahab Earning Platform, XnaksView, and the web and app development work we take on for clients.

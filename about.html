About Us

A technology company, built product by product.

Baloch Sahab Technologies started with a single earning website and grew into a small company building web platforms, mobile apps, and client work.

Our story

Baloch Sahab Technologies began as a single web project — an earning platform for everyday users. As that platform grew, so did the need for a proper company behind it: one that could take on a mobile app, take on client projects, and be accountable under its own legal name.

In August 2026, the company was formally incorporated in Pakistan under the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) as an SMC-Private Limited company — a single-member company limited by shares. Today it operates as the parent company behind our earning platform, our Android app EarnBox, and the web/app development work we take on for clients.

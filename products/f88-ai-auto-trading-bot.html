F88 AI Auto Trading Bot

An automated, risk-managed trading assistant for MetaTrader 5 accounts.

Overview

F88 AI Auto Trading Bot pairs a Flutter app with a Python/FastAPI backend that connects to real MetaTrader 5 (MT5) broker accounts, such as Exness.

A confluence signal engine produces Buy, Sell or Hold signals with a confidence score and reasons. Every automatic trade passes through a single risk-management gate before it is sent.

F88 is in development and is currently self-hosted by its operator. It is not offered as a public or multi-customer service.

Key features

  • Connect one or more MetaTrader 5 accounts, each in its own isolated terminal
  • Signal engine: Buy / Sell / Hold with confidence and reasons
  • Position sizing from real symbol tick values (risk % per trade)
  • Every trade sent with stop-loss and take-profit (ATR-based or fixed pips)
  • Daily loss limit, max open trades and minimum-confidence guards
  • Explicit confirmation required before auto-trading on a live (non-demo) account
  • Live balance, equity, positions and trade history with win-rate stats
  • Broker passwords encrypted at rest

Risk warning: trading foreign exchange, metals and CFDs carries a high level of risk and may not be suitable for everyone. Automated trading does not guarantee profit and you can lose all of your capital. Nothing on this website is financial advice.

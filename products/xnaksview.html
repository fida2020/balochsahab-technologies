XnaksView

A short-video and LIVE streaming social app for creators and their audiences.

Overview

XnaksView is a social video app for Android. Viewers scroll a feed of short videos and join creators in LIVE rooms, where they can chat and send virtual gifts.

Creators can go LIVE, invite guests on stage and earn from gifts received. The app also includes one-to-one direct messages and group chats.

XnaksView is currently in development and has not yet been publicly released on Google Play.

Key features

  • Short-video feed with comments
  • LIVE streaming with multi-guest rooms
  • Virtual gifts and in-app coins (Google Play Billing)
  • Creator earnings with identity verification (KYC) before withdrawal
  • Direct messages and group chats with GIFs and stickers
  • Follow system, profiles and notifications

Not yet available to download. Public release on Google Play is planned.

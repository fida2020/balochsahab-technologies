Overview

The Baloch Sahab Earning Platform is where the company started. It is a reward-based web platform that lets everyday users earn small amounts by completing tasks, viewing ads and engaging daily.

The platform is built, operated and supported in-house by Baloch Sahab Technologies, including payouts and user support.

Key features

  • PTC (paid-to-click) ads
  • Offerwalls
  • Daily faucet
  • Spin & win
  • Daily check-ins
  • Referral program
  • Real wallet balances and withdrawals

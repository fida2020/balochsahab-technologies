Our Android earning app: rewarded ads, offers, daily bonus and real withdrawals.

Overview

EarnBox is our first step into mobile. It brings the reward model of the Baloch Sahab Earning Platform to a native Android application.

Users can watch rewarded ads, complete offers and claim a daily bonus, then withdraw their earnings to JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank transfer or USDT.

Key features

  • Rewarded ads
  • Offer completion
  • Daily bonus
  • Withdrawals to JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank transfer or USDT
